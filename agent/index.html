    When a person takes over

    The agent answers what the studio has already decided: hours, addresses, booking, piece status, event basics. Anything that needs judgement goes to the team, with a summary, so the guest never has to repeat the story.

    • ComplaintsHanded over at once, with the guest's words and order details.
    • Refunds and payment disputesThe agent never promises money back or a discount.
    • Changes a link can't handleMoving a group, splitting a bill, special requests on a booking.
    • A photo of a damaged pieceCracks, chips or glaze problems go straight to the team with the photo.
    • Large corporate eventsThe agent collects the basics; the team sends the quote.
    • The guest asks for a person"אפשר לדבר עם נציג?" is honoured on the first ask.
    • Two unclear messages in a rowAfter two misses, the agent stops guessing and hands over.
    • Outside opening hours and on Shabbat

      The agent still answers the simple questions. For everything else it collects the request and promises a reply at the next opening hour.
