Two ways we can work together

Dmitry Khinkis · 2 October 2026

The roadmap is the same either way: four steps, one at a time, so we can make sure each one works well before moving on. The difference is simply how you prefer to structure the work and payment.

The four steps

  1. 1AI assistant for WhatsApp~4 weeks₪8,000Guests simply write as they do today. The AI answers common questions, uses Color Café's information, and hands the conversation to the right branch when a person is needed.Done means: the assistant answers the agreed topics on your WhatsApp number, and chats that need a person reach the right branch with a short summary.
  2. 2Piece tracking in one branch~4 weeks₪8,000We start with one branch and test the full journey in real life: drop-off, kiln, ready and pickup. Once it works smoothly, guests automatically get a message when their piece is ready.Done means: in one branch every new piece is tracked from drop-off to pickup, and guests get the “ready” message.
  3. 3All branches and a simple dashboard~3 weeks₪5,500Tracking expands to every branch, and you get one simple dashboard showing where everything is and what needs attention.Done means: every branch tracks its pieces, and the dashboard shows what is where and what needs attention.
  4. 4Warehouse for the importing business~5 weeks₪9,000You can see what came in, what is still in stock, and what was sent to branches or B2B customers.Done means: incoming stock, current stock and what went to branches and B2B customers are visible in one place.

Roughly four months in total, from mid-October to February. Timing is approximate and may shift slightly depending on access, testing and feedback along the way.

What we'll agree in writing

  • What "done" means. Each step has a short list, like the ones above. A step is finished when those points work.

  • Order of steps. Step 3 builds on step 2. Steps 1 and 4 can be done on their own, in any order.
  • If you choose monthly. Around 10 hours a week; unused hours don't carry over. You set the week's priorities, replies come within one working day, and anything beyond the limit moves to the next week.
