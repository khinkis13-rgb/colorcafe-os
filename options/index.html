The road is the same either way: four steps, one at a time, so each one really works before we start the next. The only difference is how you'd like to pay.

The four steps

  1. 1AI on WhatsApp instead of Commbox~4 weeks₪5,000Guests just write, the AI answers like a person and passes the chat to the right branch when a person is needed.
  2. 2Piece tracking in one branch~4 weeks₪5,000First we test the codes in your kiln, then tracking goes live: drop-off, kiln, ready, pickup. The guest gets a message when the piece is ready.
  3. 3All branches and a simple dashboard~3 weeks₪4,000Tracking runs in every branch, and you get one screen that shows what is where and what is running late.
  4. 4Warehouse for the importing business~5 weeks₪6,000Boxes come in and get counted, and you can see what's left and what went to the branches and B2B customers.

About 4 months in total, from mid-October to February. Dates are approximate: some depend on access and answers from your side.

How to pay

Monthly
